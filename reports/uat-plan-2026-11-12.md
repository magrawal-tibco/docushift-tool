# Docs migration to AEM: testing plan around go-live (12 Nov 2026)

Go-live: 23 Nov 2026 (may move to 25–26 Nov). Hard stop for the old site: 30 Nov 2026.

## How we test

Each published product version is checked automatically on the AEM staging site, against our converted copy, which was itself checked against the original help. Every problem found is logged in Jira by kind and fixed afterwards, so testing is not held up by fixes. About 5 minutes of machine time per version; checks run overnight.

What each check covers, per version:

1. **Menu:** every page in our menu appears in the staging menu, in the same order and nesting.
2. **Pages:** every page is published on staging, none missing.
3. **Text:** every sentence of our page is shown on staging.
4. **Images:** every image is shown, identical to ours, with its alt text.
5. **Note, Tip and Warning labels** are shown.
6. **Links to headings** within and between pages land on the right heading.
7. **Old addresses:** a sample of 10 old docs.tibco.com addresses per version redirects to the right new page.
8. **Against the original:** no text, topic or menu entry was lost in conversion.
